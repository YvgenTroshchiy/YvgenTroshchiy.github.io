Cozy Work Log - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Cozy Work Log is a work day timer for iPhone and Android: one button starts and stops the timer, and the screen shows how much of your working day is left or how much overtime you have done. This policy explains what the app collects and what it does not.

What the app does not do

  • There are no accounts. The app never asks you to register or sign in.
  • It does not ask for or collect your name, e-mail, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your worked hours are never uploaded anywhere. There is no cloud sync and no server of ours that receives them.
  • It does not use the advertising identifier, does not track you across other apps or websites, shows no ads and does not sell or share data with advertisers.

Data stored on your device only

The app saves on the device: the timer state and the time worked today, your working hours per day, the illustration and theme you picked, and whether you have already answered the rating prompt. None of it is transmitted to us or to anyone else, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Data collected automatically

The app uses two Google services, on iPhone and on Android alike, to understand how it is used and to find crashes:

  • Firebase Analytics collects anonymous usage events - for example, that the timer was started or stopped, that settings were opened or that the working hours were changed - together with the technical data Google attaches to them: a randomly generated app instance identifier, device model, operating system version, app version and approximate region derived from the IP address. The advertising identifier is not collected. Test builds (TestFlight and development builds) send no analytics.
  • Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report if the app crashes: the stack trace, the device model and operating system version, and the state of the app at the moment of the crash. Crash reports contain no personal data.

Both services are provided by Google, and data is processed on Google's servers in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy; see also Firebase privacy and security. None of this data is linked to your identity or used to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Notifications and Live Activity

While the timer runs, the app shows it in a notification on Android and in a Live Activity on the iPhone Lock Screen. These are produced on the device; nothing is sent to a server to show them.

Ratings

The app may ask you to rate it. The rating itself goes through Apple's or Google's own review dialog, and the app never sees what you rate or write.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Your choices

You can stop all data collection by uninstalling the app; uninstalling also removes everything stored on the device. To request deletion of analytics or crash data associated with your device, write to the address below.
