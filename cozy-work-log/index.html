Cozy Work Log

Work your hours. Not more.

A cozy work day timer for iPhone and Android. Tap Start, let your buddy work next to you, and see how much of the day is left - or when it is time to stop.

Questions

I forgot to start the timer in the morning. Tap the timer on the main screen and type in the time you already worked; the day carries on from there.

Does the timer stop when I close the app? No. It keeps counting while the app is closed or the phone is locked, and resets to zero on a new day.

How do I change my working hours or the illustration? Open Settings with the gear icon in the top right corner.
