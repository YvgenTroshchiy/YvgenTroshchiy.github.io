1. The Service

Country Days Tracker is a travel day tracker for Android and iOS: you log trips - a country, an entry date and an exit date - and the app counts your days against the rules you choose, such as the Schengen 90/180 rule, 183-day tax residency tests, the UK’s midnight count, US physical presence or Canada’s PR and citizenship counts, and forecasts how long you may stay. The app works offline and has no user accounts. We may add, change, or remove features over time; where a change materially reduces what a paid purchase provides, we will say so in advance in the app’s release notes.

2. Not legal or tax advice

Country Days Tracker shows informational day counts against the thresholds of each rule. It is not legal, immigration or tax advice, and it never determines whether you are or are not a resident, may or may not enter a country, or owe tax anywhere.

  • Residency, visa and tax status turn on facts a trip log cannot know - ties, treaties, visas and permits, exemptions and the authorities’ own judgement.
  • Border and tax authorities rely on their own records (for example the EU Entry/Exit System, the US I-94 or Canada’s border records), which control over any count in the app.
  • Rules change. The presets reflect our reading of each rule at the time of the release you run, and some rules are simplified, as the app states where it does so.
  • Counts are only as accurate as the trips you enter. You are responsible for the dates and countries in your log.

Before relying on a count for a decision that matters - a booking, an application, a tax return - confirm your situation with a qualified professional or the competent authority.
